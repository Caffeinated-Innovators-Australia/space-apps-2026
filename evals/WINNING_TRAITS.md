# Winning traits: what Space Apps judges have rewarded

Distilled from research/past_winners_insights.md (2016–2025 winners) and updated on 14 Nov 2026 from the official
2026 Judging & Awards and Project Submission guides. Judges in the judge-evals panel read this before scoring. The `winning_traits`
checklist judge scores each item pass/fail.

Key facts for judges:
- Five criteria, each scored 1 to 5 at every level of judging (2026 guide). The guide warns that teams neglect **Presentation**, which is judged on the demo and the full project page.
- At global judging, expert judges review Global Nominees, then an executive committee from NASA and partner agencies picks winners (2026 guide). Expect a domain-expert reader.
- At least one NASA data source or resource is **required for Global Award eligibility**, and Relevance rewards NASA data used as a core component (2026 guides).
- The demo is a video of 30 s or less, or 7 or fewer slides including title and intro. **A demo over the limit is not eligible for Global Judging** (2026 guide). Most of the evaluation rests on the project page.
- Almost every 2023–25 global winner showed a working artefact (an app, dashboard, model or game), not just a concept.
- Closest analogue: SpaceGenes+ (2025, Best Use of Science). It was a dashboard over a named NASA GeneLab dataset (GLDS-288) on astronaut health.
  Astronaut vitals monitors from 2018–2022 reached Global Nominee but didn't win, so the "act" layer is what tends to be missing.
- Most losses come from admin and rules, not ideas: the "Create Your Own Challenge" track, private links, missing English subtitles,
  unregistered or waitlisted members, uncited assets, undisclosed or unmarked AI use.

"30 Seconds of Glory" (demo structure, unchanged in the 2026 Submission Guide): 0:00 who we are and which challenge · 0:02 why we chose it ·
0:05 name, tagline, problem and who gains · 0:10 how it works, with visuals and how agency data made it possible ·
0:20 the future impact · 0:30 end.


## Checklist (each item is pass/fail)

| # | Testable statement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | The submission names the official 2026 challenge it responds to, and it is not "Create Your Own Challenge". | 2021/2022 guides [Primary] |
| W2 | The "NASA Data" field names at least one NASA dataset or resource by title, ID and link and says how it was used; partner-agency data and all other sources are in "Space Agency Partner Data and Other References". | 2026 Submission Guide [Primary] |
| W3 | At least one named NASA/OSDR dataset (e.g. OSD-569, or a GeneLab study) feeds a visible element of the demo, not just the references. | SpaceGenes+ 2025 [Snippet]; [Inference] |
| W4 | The demo is either a video of 30 seconds or less or a deck of 7 slides or fewer (title and intro included), and there is exactly one demo link. | 2026 Submission Guide [Primary] |
| W5 | The demo follows the "30 Seconds of Glory" order: team and challenge, problem, solution and tagline, how it works with space data, then future impact. | 2022 Submission Guide [Primary] |
| W6 | The demo opens with the name, tagline and problem within the first 5 seconds or the first slide. | 30 Seconds of Glory [Primary] |
| W7 | The demo shows the real working software (screen capture) for at least one user journey from start to finish. | 2022 Submission Guide ("demonstration of your final project") [Primary] |
| W8 | The Final Project link (public repository or hosted app) opens in a private browser window with no sign-in or permission request. | 2022 Submission Guide [Primary] |
| W9 | All text is in English, and any non-English audio has English subtitles. | 2020–2022 guides [Primary] |
| W10 | Every team member (6 or fewer) is a confirmed, non-waitlisted participant at the same Local Event and on the Members tab, and "Submit for Judging" is pressed before 23:59 local time. | 2020–2022 guides [Primary] |
| W11 | The References field lists every external code library, image, dataset and text source, and nothing copyrighted is used without permission. | 2022 guides; 2026 Terms [Primary/Snippet] |
| W12 | The "Use of SI" field says where and how AI tools were used; AI did not supply the idea; AI images and video carry a visible watermark; AI audio, code and data are acknowledged in text and metadata; no NASA branding appears. | 2026 Submission Guide [Primary] |
| W13 | Every clinical threshold or claim shown in the app cites its source and states how strong the evidence is. | Validity criterion [Primary] |
| W14 | The project states its limitations honestly: synthetic data labelled as synthetic, sample sizes stated, no accuracy claims from synthetic tests. | Validity criterion ("Will it do what it sets out to do?") [Primary]; [Inference] |
| W15 | Each guiding question in the challenge statement maps to a visible feature, shown on the project page. | Relevance criterion ("responsive to the challenge") [Primary] |
| W16 | The tool produces an action or recommendation (for example self-care, crew medical officer review, or urgent), not just a display of values. | Shelter in Space 2020 severity-graded countermeasures [Snippet]; 2018–2022 monitor-only nominees didn't win [Inference] |
| W17 | The solution states a novel element versus existing tools in one sentence (what hasn't been tried before). | Creativity criterion [Primary] |
| W18 | The project page and demo state an Earth benefit with a named beneficiary group (for example remote and rural patients). | Impact criterion; Galactic Impact definition [Primary] |
| W19 | The project shows evidence of user-centred design: at least one persona or user test and the change it led to. | Radaway 2017 [Snippet]; Relevance "usable or user friendly" [Primary] |
| W20 | The user interface meets basic accessibility: colour is never the only alert cue, readable font sizes, plain-language text. | Global Connection (Arcobaleno 2023) [Snippet]; slide readability tip [Primary] |
| W21 | Every required 2026 field is filled in: Project Name, High-Level Summary, demo link, final project link, Detailed Description (what, how, benefits, tools), Use of SI, NASA Data, and Partner Data and Other References. | 2026 Submission Guide [Primary] |
| W22 | All judge-critical content lives on the project page itself, not only in an updates stream or external doc. | 2019 guide [Primary] |
| W23 | The demo uses strong visuals (diagrams, screenshots, the product itself) and keeps text per slide to roughly 30 words or fewer. | 2022 Submission Guide ("limit the number of words") [Primary]; the word count is [Inference] |
| W24 | A clinician-credible reviewer (team or local judge) would find no unsafe advice in the demo path. | Melbourne 2024 panel included a medical academic [Snippet]; Validity [Primary]; [Inference] |
| W25 | No recognisable name, voice, image or likeness of anyone under 18 appears anywhere in the submission, and there is no profanity. | 2026 Terms and Submission Guide [Primary] |
