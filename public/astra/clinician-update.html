Signed offEmergency steps CR-32

Seven DRSABCD steps, 30 compressions to 2 breaths, 100 to 120 a minute, pace marker at 110. At 0 g the CPR step also says "Strap the person down before you start CPR."

ChangedFirst steps follow gravity CR-38

At 0 g "Lie the person flat" is not shown. "Keep the person warm" and "Strap the person down so you can start CPR if needed" are shown in its place.

ApprovedPossible shock: first steps and systems screen CR-37 · SHK-01

New steps in our own words after ANZCOR Guideline 9.2.3: lie flat and keep warm unless already hot, control bleeding, oxygen if available and trained with a fire-risk caution, record vitals, press Emergency if not responding and not breathing normally, reassure and re-check. The crew medic gets a four-heading checklist (blood or fluid loss, heart, widened blood vessels, blockage) and a RUSH scan step if trained.

Signed offMeasured fever counts as a red flag CR-58 · VP-01

A temperature of 38.0 °C or more in the latest vital signs counts as "Fever or chills" in the kidney pathway. Logging fever or chills with no vital signs since shows "Record your vital signs".

Signed offUrine strip notes CR-41

Blood, nitrite and leucocytes present: "possible infection". Nitrite and leucocytes present: "possible infection". The blood-only line is kept.

Signed offGuided ultrasound CR-42

New "look for" wording, a rescan after emptying the bladder if both sides look swollen, and five drawn kidney examples in the long view: normal, then grades 1 to 4. The drawings are simple shapes made in code. The app never reads a real image.
