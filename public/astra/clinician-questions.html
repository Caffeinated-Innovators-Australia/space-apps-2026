Questions for our clinicians

The app uses placeholder rules until a clinician confirms them. This form lists every open question, 57 in all. Section 1 holds the twelve that matter most today. The rest can wait.

How to answer
  1. For each question tap OK as is, Change it or Not sure. Add a note only where you want a change.
  2. Skip anything outside your field. Unanswered questions are left out.
  3. Tap Copy my answers at the bottom and paste them into the team chat.

Your answers save on this device as you go. Nothing is sent anywhere until you paste it. Nothing here is a diagnosis or advice for a real patient. Every crew value in the app is made up.

We show a role on screen, never a name.
