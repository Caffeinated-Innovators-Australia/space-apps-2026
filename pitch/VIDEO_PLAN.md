# Demo video plan

The current `demo-draft.mp4` (29.1 seconds, 1920 by 1080, no sound) predates this plan. It shows the old single-blood-count story and will be recut. The final cut follows the official 2026 suggested outline, uses real screen capture of the new app, and carries our doctor's voice, the team's own story (with the family's agreement, and no child's name, image or voice) and the chosen project name.

## Draft voice-over for the team to rewrite (about 65 words)

> [Doctor, in their own words: one sentence on the night, how far from a hospital.] On Mars, no one is flying in, and a reply from Earth can take 45 minutes. On Day 87, our tool spots a slow slide in sleep and rising cabin carbon dioxide, asks for a quick reaction test, shows the signals together, and counts down to Earth's reply. The same tool can serve remote Australia.

## Before the final cut

- Keep the total at 29.5 seconds or less. Over 30 seconds makes the project ineligible for global judging.
- Name the NASA data on screen: "Data: NASA OSDR, OSD-569", NASA DONKI and NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2. Keep the SYNTHETIC labels visible.
- Host the final video at a public link that needs no sign-in, and put that link in the project page.
- Log every third-party asset in REFERENCES.md. No NASA logos.
