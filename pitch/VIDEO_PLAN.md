# Demo video plan

`demo-draft.mp4` is a draft cut: 29.1 seconds, 1920 by 1080, no sound. It is under the 30-second limit. It was assembled with AI assistance from a real screen capture of our live app plus text cards, and carries a small "Draft cut, assembled with AI assistance" mark. The team replaces it with the final cut: our doctor's voice, the team's own story (with the family's agreement, and no child's name, image or voice), and the chosen project name.

## Draft voice-over for the team to rewrite (about 65 words)

> [Doctor, in their own words: one sentence on the night, how far from a hospital.] On the way to Mars, no one is flying in. A question to Earth can take 45 minutes to come back. Our tool compares each reading with the astronaut's own baseline, says what it means and what to do now, and counts down to Earth's reply. The same care, for crews and for remote Australia.
