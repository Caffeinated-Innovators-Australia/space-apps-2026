# Clinician review list

Nothing here is clinical advice until a clinician signs it off. The build uses each item as a visibly marked placeholder until then.

| # | Item | Placeholder in the build | Source found | Status |

| CR-01 | Which indicators the daily check-in uses | Fifteen indicators in seven groups, added at the team lead's request (D-015). Mind and sleep: Sleep (h), Mood (1 to 5), Reaction time (ms, a tap test inside the app, about thirty seconds). Heart: Resting heart rate (bpm), Blood pressure upper and lower (mmHg, weekly). Eyes: Near vision (lines read, worse eye, weekly). Muscle and bone: Exercise plan done (%), Grip strength (kg, weekly), Body mass (kg, weekly). Food and fluid: Fluid intake (L), Food energy (kcal). Blood: Haemoglobin (g/dL, weekly). Cabin and radiation: Cabin carbon dioxide (mmHg, from a cabin sensor) and Radiation dose (mSv, a running total from a personal dosimeter), both SYNTHETIC in the demo. The team's challenge brief advises five to seven indicators. The other three blood counts stay on the "Real crew data" screen only | Team's challenge brief; OSD-569 for haemoglobin; NASA-STD-3001 Volume 1 for the strength floor and the career radiation limit | Needs clinician decision |
| CR-02 | The rule set: how far from personal baseline counts as Self-manage, Crew medic or Urgent | Band = own baseline mean plus or minus 2 standard deviations of own baseline (for haemoglobin, the variation from OSD-569). PB-01 level: one reading outside the band is amber. TR-01 trend: the average of the last 7 readings has moved more than 1 standard deviation from baseline in the direction that matters, amber, even when every reading is inside the band. SU-01 sustained: the last 3 readings all outside the band is red. LM-01 limit: one reading outside the band and beyond a sourced limit (NASA cabin carbon dioxide limit of 3 mmHg, the NASA strength floor of 80% of the person's own baseline for grip strength, or the laboratory range for haemoglobin) is red; a radiation total over the 600 mSv career limit is also red. CM-01 combined: two or more flagged indicators on one day are shown together on one card. PR-01 prompt: a flagged sleep or cabin carbon dioxide result asks for a reaction-time check | NASA OSDR, OSD-569 for haemoglobin variation and ranges; NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2 for the carbon dioxide limit; NASA-STD-3001 Volume 1 for the strength floor and career radiation limit; the factor of 2 and the counts are statistical conventions. No published space-specific alert bands were found | Needs clinician decision |
