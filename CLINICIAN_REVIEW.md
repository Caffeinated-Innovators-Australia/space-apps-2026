| CR-01 | Which indicators the daily check-in uses | Fifteen indicators in seven groups, added at the team lead's request (D-015). Mind and sleep: Sleep (h), Mood (1 to 5), Reaction time (ms, a tap test inside the app, about thirty seconds). Heart: Resting heart rate (bpm), Blood pressure upper and lower (mmHg, weekly). Eyes: Near vision (lines read, worse eye, weekly). Muscle and bone: Exercise plan done (%), Grip strength (kg, weekly), Body mass (kg, weekly). Food and fluid: Fluid intake (L), Food energy (kcal). | CR-02 | The rule set: how far from personal baseline counts as Nothing to do, Watch or Act now | Band = own baseline mean plus or minus 2 standard deviations of own baseline (for haemoglobin, the variation from OSD-569). PB-01 level: one reading outside the band is amber. TR-01 trend: the average of the last 7 readings has moved more than 1 standard deviation from baseline in the direction that matters, amber, even when every reading is inside the band. SU-01 sustained: the last 3 readings all outside the band is red.
