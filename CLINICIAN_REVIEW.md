# Clinician review list

Nothing here is clinical advice until a clinician signs it off. The build uses each item as a visibly marked placeholder until then.

| # | Item | Placeholder in the build | Source found | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CR-01 | Which indicators the daily check-in uses | Six indicators, chosen from the team's challenge brief list: Sleep (h), Resting heart rate (bpm), Mood (1 to 5), Reaction time (ms, a tap test inside the app, about thirty seconds), Cabin carbon dioxide (mmHg, from a cabin sensor, SYNTHETIC in the demo) and Haemoglobin (g/dL, weekly). The other three blood counts stay on the "Real crew data" screen only | Team's challenge brief; OSD-569 for haemoglobin | Needs clinician decision |
| CR-02 | The rule set: how far from personal baseline counts as Self-manage, Crew medic or Urgent | Band = own baseline mean plus or minus 2 standard deviations of own baseline (for haemoglobin, the variation from OSD-569). PB-01 level: one reading outside the band is amber. TR-01 trend: the average of the last 7 readings has moved more than 1 standard deviation from baseline in the direction that matters, amber, even when every reading is inside the band. SU-01 sustained: the last 3 readings all outside the band is red. LM-01 limit: one reading outside the band and beyond a sourced limit (NASA cabin carbon dioxide limit of 3 mmHg, or the laboratory range for haemoglobin) is red. CM-01 combined: two or more flagged indicators on one day are shown together on one card. PR-01 prompt: a flagged sleep or cabin carbon dioxide result asks for a reaction-time check | NASA OSDR, OSD-569 for haemoglobin variation and ranges; NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2 for the carbon dioxide limit; the factor of 2 and the counts are statistical conventions. No published space-specific alert bands were found | Needs clinician decision |
| CR-03 | Explanation card wording for the six indicators: how to measure, why it matters, what to do if out of range, the hazards and the sources; also the "Why this alert?" line | Draft text is on screen, taken from the team's challenge brief, with a "Draft wording, awaiting clinician sign-off" note and no review badge. Why-alert lines state only the numbers | Team's challenge brief; NASA HRP hazards and risks pages | Needs clinician wording |
| CR-04 | The numbered steps for each tier, and the "meanwhile" steps on the gap screen | Generic steps written for the build. Self-manage: log the reading; check in again at the next scheduled time. Crew medic: repeat the reading; tell your crew medic; send the case to Earth. Urgent: tell your crew medic now; repeat the reading; send the case to Earth | To be written by clinicians | Needs clinician wording |
| CR-05 | Units for display (for example haemoglobin in g/L, the Australian convention, or g/dL as in OSD-569) | As in source data | OSD-569 uses g/dL | Needs clinician decision |
| CR-06 | Role wording for the review badge ("Reviewed by our ...") | No badge shown | Team design system | Needs clinician decision |
| CR-07 | Laboratory reference ranges for haemoglobin by sex (and for the other three blood counts on the real crew data screen) | The ranges the source laboratory reported in OSD-569; each synthetic crew member has a sex field only to pick the range | NASA OSDR, OSD-569 (LSDS-7) | Needs clinician decision |
| CR-08 | Confidence label for the rule (High, Medium, Low) | Low | Team design system | Needs clinician decision |

| CR-12 | Whether a tier may be raised on one reading, or needs a repeat reading first | One reading outside the band is amber; three in a row is red; one reading beyond a sourced limit is also red (LM-01). | CR-13 | Privacy policy defaults: the "Who sees what" table for you, crew medic, flight surgeon and rest of crew | Draft table on screen, marked as a draft policy for the team to confirm | Team design choice | Needs clinician decision |

| CR-15 | The mood scale words | 1 to 5: Very low, Low, OK, Good, Very good | Team design choice | Needs clinician decision |
| CR-16 | The trend, sustained and combined rule numbers: the 7-reading window, the shift of 1 standard deviation, the run of 3, and what a combined picture should do | Window 7, shift 1 standard deviation, run of 3, combined minimum of 2. The combined card takes the highest tier among the flagged signals and does not change any single result | Statistical conventions; no published space-specific figures found | Needs clinician decision |
| CR-17 | Whether the rules should change by environment (Mars base, In transit, Moon base, Space station, Remote Australia) | The rules are the same in every environment. Only the reply delay, who answers, the dominant risks and the guidance line change | Team design choice | Needs clinician decision |
| CR-18 | The tier words. The challenge brief says amber means "watch or retest" and red means "act now, contact flight surgeon". The design system words are "Crew medic" and "Urgent" | Green Self-manage, amber Crew medic, red Urgent. The team should pick one meaning | Team's challenge brief; team design system | Needs clinician decision |

All of the above live in `rules/rules.yaml` and `src/data/seed.json` (the seed file is generated by `scripts/make_mission_data.py`). To sign a rule off, replace `signed_off_by: placeholder` with the reviewing clinician's role.
