# Data dictionary

| Dataset | ID | Link | Licence | What we use | Real / SYNTHETIC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NASA Open Science Data Repository, Inspiration4 blood study | OSD-569 (file LSDS-7, Complete Blood Count) | https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-569 | NASA open data (CC0 unless marked); cited | Complete blood count for four crew at three pre-launch and four post-return timepoints, with laboratory reference ranges. The within-person variation of the pre-launch haemoglobin draws sets the width of the haemoglobin band, and the haemoglobin laboratory ranges set its limit. All four blood counts are drawn on the "Real crew data" screen, each with its own band width from the same pre-launch variation | Real |
| NASA DONKI, solar energetic particle (SEP) events | DONKI SEP | https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/ | NASA public data; cited | Solar particle events bundled at build time. The latest event is shown on the space-weather card. Events that fall inside the mission window are listed in the mission data as radiation prompt days | Real |
| NASA technical standard, cabin carbon dioxide limit | NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2 | none held in the repo | NASA published standard; cited | One number: the one-hour average cabin carbon dioxide limit of 3 mmHg. It is a habitat design requirement, not a clinical alert threshold | Real |
| NASA technical standard, crew health | NASA-STD-3001 Volume 1 | none held in the repo | NASA published standard; cited | Two numbers: in-mission skeletal muscle strength at or above 80% of baseline (used as the grip strength floor, 80% of each person's own baseline), and the career effective dose limit of 600 mSv from spaceflight (used for radiation dose). Both are programme requirements, not clinical alert thresholds | Real |
| Simulated mission, four crew (`src/data/seed.json`) | none | made by the team with `scripts/make_mission_data.py` (fixed seed 2026) | Apache 2.0 | Fifteen indicators in seven groups for four crew members: a pre-flight baseline (14 days for daily indicators and reaction time; 4 readings for blood pressure, grip strength and body mass; 4 near-vision results; 3 draws for haemoglobin; the first two mission weeks for cabin carbon dioxide; none for radiation dose) and 86 mission days, with day 87 as today. Weekly indicators have a value only on their own day of the mission week. Radiation dose is a running total. Also today's demo values for crew member 1, and the fixed configuration: indicator definitions and explanation cards, five environment profiles, the crew timeline (with the 45-minute EVA slip), the symptom list and the privacy table | SYNTHETIC values; real haemoglobin variation and ranges, real carbon dioxide limit, real strength floor and career radiation limit, real radiation prompt days |

Notes: the app's fifteen indicators, by group, are: Mind and sleep: sleep (h), mood (1 to 5), reaction time (ms). Heart: resting heart rate (bpm), blood pressure upper and lower (mmHg). Eyes: near vision (lines read, 0 to 8, worse eye). Muscle and bone: exercise plan done (%), grip strength (kg), body mass (kg). Food and fluid: fluid intake (L), food energy (kcal). Blood: haemoglobin (g/dL). Cabin and radiation: cabin carbon dioxide (mmHg), radiation dose (mSv, mission total). Cabin carbon dioxide and radiation dose come from a cabin sensor and a personal dosimeter in a real system; in the demo both are SYNTHETIC and no device is connected. Near-vision results are SYNTHETIC in the mission data; on the device they come from the self-check, which is not a calibrated eye chart. The other three blood counts (haematocrit, red cells, white cells) appear only on the "Real crew data" screen. OSD-569 has no in-flight draws. Timepoints are L-92, L-44, L-3 (days before launch) and R+1, R+45, R+82, R+194 (days after return). To change the simulated data, edit `scripts/make_mission_data.py` and run it again; do not edit `seed.json` by hand.
